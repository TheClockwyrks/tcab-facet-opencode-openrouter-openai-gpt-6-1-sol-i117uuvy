# Facet

Every stone has a breaking point. Take your place at the cutting bench and swap neighboring gems into lines of three or more. Watch them fracture, tumble, and set off another beautiful chain reaction.

The stones left standing remember. Each clear puts pressure on its neighbors, deepening their cracks until they become flawed. Touch that primed corner with your next clear and the whole seam can give way—each flawed stone is worth double, and every step of a chain raises the stakes.

A careful cut leaves something precious behind. Match four to earn a brilliant that takes its surrounding ring. Cross two lines for a star that opens an entire row and column. Line up five for a turning prism, then trade it against a stone to shatter every gem of that kind.

Meet each level’s growing target, admire your longest chain and best move, then take on a fresh field. There is no clock to race: just seven stones, the pressure you build, and the next cut you choose. Keep going until no move remains.
